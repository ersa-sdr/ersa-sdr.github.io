Frequency axis

With sample rate $f_s$, FFT length $N$ and tuned centre frequency $f_c$, each display bin at baseband frequency $f_{\text{bb}}$ maps to RF as

$$f_{\text{label}} = f_c + f_{\text{bb}}, \qquad -\frac{f_s}{2} \le f_{\text{bb}} < \frac{f_s}{2}, \qquad \Delta f = \frac{f_s}{N}$$

For $f_s = 2.6\,\text{MS/s}$ and $N = 1024$, the bin spacing is $\Delta f \approx 2.54\,\text{kHz}$.
